# Internal representations do not entail understanding

Linear probes showing structured internal representations demonstrate that LLMs encode useful statistical regularities, not that they understand anything. A thermometer encodes temperature as mercury height — a structured representation — without understanding temperature. The presence of isomorphic internal structure is evidence of effective compression, not comprehension. Understanding requires the ability to use representations in open-ended reasoning, not merely to encode them.
