# LLMs are stochastic parrots performing statistical pattern matching

LLMs are trained to predict the next token in a sequence. Their outputs are the product of conditional probability distributions over token sequences, not of any process that involves forming or manipulating concepts. What appears as understanding is compression of training data into interpolation patterns. The system has no referents, no world model, and no intentions — only conditional distributions.
