# Novel problem solving in LLMs is confounded by data contamination

Claims of novel problem solving are undermined by the impossibility of verifying that specific inputs were absent from training data. LLMs are trained on vast internet corpora that include solutions to most benchmark problems and their variations. What appears as generalization may be sophisticated interpolation within a training distribution so large that its boundaries are unknown. Without guaranteed novelty, behavioral evidence is systematically unreliable.
