# Statistical learning at sufficient scale produces genuine representations

The stochastic parrot framing assumes that statistical pattern matching cannot give rise to semantic content. But emergent capabilities at scale demonstrate that next-token prediction, applied to enough data with enough parameters, produces internal structures that function as world models. The linear probe literature shows that LLMs develop spatial, temporal, and causal representations — these are not mere token correlations but structured abstractions.
