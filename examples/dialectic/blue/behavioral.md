# The Chinese Room argument proves too much

If behavioral competence on genuinely novel inputs does not count as understanding, then we have no grounds for attributing understanding to other humans either — since we only ever observe their behavior. The Chinese Room proves too much: it would deny understanding to any system evaluated purely from the outside. In practice, systematic generalization to novel problems is our best available evidence of understanding, and LLMs exhibit it.
